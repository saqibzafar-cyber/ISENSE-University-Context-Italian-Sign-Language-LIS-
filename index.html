Overview

The ISENSE Dataset is an Italian Sign Language (LIS) dataset acquired using RGB cameras. Thirteen LIS experts participated in this study, including seven males (age: 43.4 ± 15.8 years) and six females (age: 40.1 ± 10.7 years).

Eleven participants were deaf LIS users, including seven males and four females, while the remaining two participants were professional LIS interpreters. All participants were right-hand dominant and reported no physical impairment affecting hand or finger function.

Prior to data acquisition, each participant received detailed information on the target isolated signs and phrases from a professional LIS expert. Informed consent was obtained from all participants in accordance with the 1964 Declaration of Helsinki. The study protocol was approved by the Ethical Committee of the University of Tuscia (No. 22122024).
